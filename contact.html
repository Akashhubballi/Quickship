Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find fast answers to common questions about pickups, delivery windows, insurance, and tracking.

How does QuickShip Same-Day pickup work?
When you submit a Same-Day courier booking before 2:00 PM, an express rider is dispatched to your pickup address within 30 minutes. The courier collects the parcel, scans the barcode label on their mobile terminal, and drives directly to the recipient address without intermediate depot sorting delays.
What happens if the recipient is unavailable at delivery?
Our courier will make up to three delivery attempts. If no one is available and no safe-drop instructions were provided, the parcel is held at the nearest secure QuickShip Hub, and an automated SMS notification with redelivery rescheduling options is immediately sent to the recipient.
Is package insurance included with my booking?
Yes! All Standard Ground deliveries include automatic transit coverage up to $100. Express Air Priority packages are covered up to $1,000, and Same-Day consignments are insured up to $500. Additional declared-value coverage can be requested during booking.
How do I track my shipment without an account?
No account creation or login is needed. Simply enter your Air Waybill (AWB) consignment number (e.g., QS-8829-US) in our search bar on the Home or Tracking page to see live checkpoint scans, driver GPS coordinates, and arrival estimates.
Are there restrictions on package dimensions or prohibited goods?
Standard couriers can transport items up to 30 kg and 120 cm length. For larger consignments, our Business Freight tier provides lift-gate trucks. We do not transport hazardous combustibles, illegal substances, or perishable food requiring refrigeration without prior contract agreement.
